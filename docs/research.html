How accurate are betting odds?

I tested betting odds on 52,841 matches from the Bundesliga, English Premier League, English Championship, Ligue 1, Serie A and La Liga, every season since 2000.

See the findingsThis week's best bets

What I found.

  • 01The odds are accurate
  • 02Long shots are the worst bet
  • 03Bookmakers take a smaller cut than they used to
  • 04Long shots stayed the worse bet as margins fell
  • 05Prices that move before kick-off move the right way
  • 06A model built from past results can't beat the odds
  • 07The second division is priced as accurately as the first
  • 08After half-time, the team that's behind scores more than expected

FINDING 5

Prices that move before kick-off move the right way

When the odds shortened before kick-off (chance up 3+ points), a $100 bet at the opening odds made +$11 on average. When they drifted, it lost $15.

Betting early pays off when the price later moves your way

FINDING 8

After half-time, the team that's behind scores more than expected

Teams losing by 2+ at half-time scored 15% more in the second half than the odds predicted. Teams winning by 2+ scored 5% fewer.

Slight favorites leading 1–0 at half-time were given a 76% chance to win and won 72% of the time (10,249 matches). Games level at half-time were given a 38% chance of a draw and ended level 40% of the time.

In the second half, the team that was behind was expected to score 43.7% of the goals and scored 47.0%. This held in 12 of 12 checks.

How it's built.

  • Data. Results and odds from up to ten bookmakers per match, from football-data.co.uk.
  • Removing the margin. Three methods compared: proportional, power and Shin's 1993 model.
  • Model. Team ratings refitted every week on past matches only, in scikit-learn.
  • Uncertainty. 95% ranges from resampling whole matches (the bootstrap).
  • Tests. Automated checks, including one that the model never sees a match before predicting it.
  • Odds. Snapshots from European bookmakers collected by football-data.co.uk.

Odds from The Odds API. Match history from football-data.co.uk. Prices move constantly, so check the site before acting.
