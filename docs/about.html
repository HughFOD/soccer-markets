About Today's Soccer Bets.

A free daily list of soccer bets where one site's odds are better than all the sites together say they should be.

How a bet is chosen.

Collect the odds.

Every site's odds for every bet, through The Odds API. Prediction-market prices are converted to what you get after their trading fee (Kalshi 0.07 × p × (1−p) per $1 contract, Polymarket 0.05 × p × (1−p)).

Work out what's expected.

Each site's odds become chances with its cut removed, then are averaged with equal weight. That gives every outcome's expected chance.

Find the bets that beat it.

A bet is listed when a site's odds pay more than its expected chance deserves. Gaps over 25% are left out as likely stale or mistyped prices.

What's covered.

  • Sportsbooks: FanDuel, DraftKings, Fanatics, BetMGM, Caesars.
  • Prediction markets: Kalshi and Polymarket.
  • Competitions: Premier League, Championship, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1, Champions League, Europa League, FA Cup, DFB-Pokal, Copa del Rey, Coppa Italia, Coupe de France, Carabao Cup, Conference League.
  • Bets: the match result and total goals for every match. On match day, corners, cards, player bets and more are added.
  • Updates: the odds are refreshed every morning, New York time. Once a match kicks off, it disappears from the lists.

Keeping score.

  • Every bet listed on the homepage is saved at the price first shown.
  • Before kick-off it's checked again against the latest odds. Still being a good bet at kick-off is the fairest test, because by then the news is in.
  • After the match it's settled from the final score, assuming $100 on each bet. Corners and cards in the six leagues are settled from each game's statistics. Player bets aren't counted, because there's no free record of who scored or shot.
  • Every result is on the Results page, where you can filter by won, lost, league or site.

Bet responsibly.

This is a study of pricing, not betting advice. A bet that beats what's expected still loses often.

You must be of legal age (21 in most states) and in a state where sports betting is legal.

If gambling stops being fun, call or text 1-800-GAMBLER.
